# Microsoft Copilot in PowerPoint

**Descriacption**

Gone are the days of spending hours switching between documents and writing your PowerPoint presentations from scratch with Microsoft Copilot. In this course, you'll use Copilot embedded in PowerPoint to build engaging presentations, derive slide decks from reference files, design interesting slide layouts, and even generate speaker notes. Discover how you can spend less time writing presentations and slide decks with Microsoft Copilot today!
